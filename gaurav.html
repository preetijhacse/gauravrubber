About Gaurav Rubber Udyog

Precision Manufacturing for Industrial Rubber Solutions

Established with a commitment to engineering precision, Gaurav Rubber Udyog is a leading manufacturer and exporter of high-durability Rubber Beadings, Rubber Cords, Extruded Rubber Profiles, Silicone Gaskets, Hose Pipes, and Moulded Components based in Faridabad (NCR, India).

Our modern continuous vulcanization (CV) extrusion setups and precision compression molding facilities allow us to synthesize specialized elastomers including EPDM, Silicone, Nitrile (NBR), Neoprene (CR), Natural Rubber (NR), and Viton (FKM) according to client CAD drawings and custom Shore hardness specifications.
