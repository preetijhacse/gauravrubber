Plant Capabilities

State-of-the-Art Production Facility

Equipped with heavy hydraulic compression molding, cold-feed continuous extrusion, and automated cryo-deflashing lines.

Compression & Transfer Molding

Tonnage range from 50 Tons to 500 Tons PLC-controlled hydraulic presses capable of molding high-precision seals, bellows, and rubber-to-metal bonded mounts.

Continuous Profile Extrusion

Microwave continuous vulcanization (LCM/HAV) lines for dual-durometer sponge and solid EPDM profiles, co-extruded with metal carrier strips.

In-House Tooling & Die Lab

High-precision CNC wire-cut machines and CAD/CAM software to manufacture multi-cavity hardened steel molds with 0.01mm tolerance accuracy.
